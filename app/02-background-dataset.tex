\chapter{Phase-I Background Dataset}
\label{chap:phase1-background-dataset}

This thesis focuses extensively on the analysis of Auger's Phase-II data that is
measured with AugerPrime electronics. In some instances, a verification, 
especially of \UUB-reconstructed simulated air showers, is needed.

Prior validation studies have shown that there exist no systematic differences 
between Phase-I and Phase-II data, both in reconstructed station-level 
observables (Ref.~\cite{payerasCompatibilityUBUUB2023}), as well as event-level 
discriminators (Ref.~\todo{ref}). The Phase-I dataset, representing a large catalog of observed \CR events measured with \UB electronics is thus an excellent candidate to verify some simulation driven variables using measured data. 

The following pages detail the selection, reconstruction and processing of the 
background dataset that is referenced throughout the analysis chapters.
