\section{Monte Carlo Datasets}

Confirmed photon \CRs, detected e.g.~by the \LHAASO have reconstructed primary 
energies of $<\SI{10}{\peta\eV}$ 
\cite{lhaasocollaborationUltrahighenergyRayBubble2024}, less than 
\SI{10}{\percent} of the lower bound on the energy considered in this work. 
No photon candidates have ever been measured in the search space studied here.

With this lack of experimental evidence we must base our analysis on 
simulations of \EASs. The following sections detail how these simulations are 
performed, and present the datasets of simulated air showers used throughout 
\cref{chap:chapter-4}.
